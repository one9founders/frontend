Role-Specific Questionnaires
After completing basic details, users proceed to role-specific questions based on their selected track. Each track has 3-5 questions designed to evaluate skills, thinking process, and hunger to learn (not prior experience).
🎯 PRODUCT TRACK - Questions
Question 1: Website Audit
Prompt: "Visit one9founders.com and spend 10 minutes exploring the platform. List 3-5 things that feel confusing, broken, or could be improved from a user's perspective. Be specific about where you found each issue."
How to answer:
* Be specific: Instead of "Search is bad," say "Search doesn't show results for partial keywords like 'prod' for 'productivity'"
* Explain the problem: Why does this matter to users?
* Give context: Where did you encounter this? What were you trying to do?
Format: Text area (1000 characters max)
Question 2: Feature Prioritization
Prompt: "Imagine you're the product manager. We can only build ONE new feature in the next month. What should it be and why? Consider: user needs, business impact, and feasibility."
How to answer:
* Name the feature clearly
* Explain who needs it and why
* Think about impact: How many users? How often will they use it?
* Consider trade-offs: What are you NOT building to prioritize this?
Format: Text area (800 characters max)
Question 3: Competitor Analysis
Prompt: "Find 2-3 other AI tools directories or similar platforms. What do they do better than us? What do we do better than them?"
How to answer:
* Name the competitors you researched
* Be honest about what they do better
* Highlight our strengths
* Suggest: What should we learn from them?
Format: Text area (1000 characters max)
Question 4: User Story
Prompt: "Describe a time when you were frustrated using a website or app. What went wrong? How would you have fixed it if you were building it?"
How to answer:
* Tell a specific story (not generic frustrations)
* Explain what you were trying to accomplish
* Describe your solution in practical terms
* Show empathy: How would this help other users?
Format: Text area (600 characters max)

🎨 UI/UX TRACK - Questions
Question 1: First Impressions
Prompt: "Visit one9founders.com for the first time. Within 30 seconds, what do you understand about what we do? What's confusing? What caught your attention first?"
How to answer:
* Describe your first 30 seconds honestly
* What did your eyes naturally go to?
* Could you immediately tell what we do?
* What questions did you have?
Format: Text area (800 characters max)
Question 2: User Flow Analysis
Prompt: "Try to complete this task on our website: Find an AI tool for email marketing, compare it with 2 other similar tools, then save it for later. Describe each step. Where did you get stuck or feel frustrated?"
How to answer:
* Walk through each step you took
* Point out friction: Where did you hesitate? Where did you click the wrong thing?
* Suggest improvements: How would you make this easier?
* Be specific about button placements, labels, visual hierarchy
Format: Text area (1200 characters max)
Question 3: Mobile Experience
Prompt: "Open our website on your phone. What works well? What doesn't? How would you improve the mobile experience?"
How to answer:
* Test on actual mobile device (not desktop browser)
* Comment on: Touch targets, text readability, navigation, scrolling
* Compare to good mobile experiences you've had
* Be specific about what feels awkward or smooth
Format: Text area (800 characters max)
Question 4: Wireframe Redesign (Optional Bonus)
Prompt: "Sketch or describe a redesigned homepage for One9Founders. What would you change? You can draw on paper, use Figma, or just describe it in detail."
How to answer:
* Focus on 2-3 major changes (not a complete redesign)
* Explain WHY each change improves the user experience
* You can upload a sketch image or describe in words
Format: Text area (1000 chars) OR Image upload

✨ DESIGN TRACK - Questions
Question 1: Instagram Audit
Prompt: "Visit @one9founders on Instagram. Scroll through our last 10 posts. What works visually? What doesn't grab your attention? What would you change?"
How to answer:
* Comment on: Color scheme, typography, visual consistency, composition
* Compare specific posts: Which ones stand out? Why?
* Think about Instagram feed view: Does it look cohesive?
* Suggest 2-3 specific visual improvements
Format: Text area (1000 characters max)
Question 2: Brand Identity Analysis
Prompt: "Look at our website and Instagram together. What personality/vibe does our brand give off? Is it consistent? What emotion should users feel when they see our brand?"
How to answer:
* Describe the current vibe in 3-5 adjectives (e.g., modern, playful, serious, techy)
* Is website and Instagram consistent in tone?
* What do you think we WANT users to feel?
* Suggest how to strengthen brand identity
Format: Text area (800 characters max)
Question 3: Visual Hierarchy
Prompt: "Look at our homepage hero section. What do your eyes naturally look at first, second, third? Is this the order we WANT users to look? How would you guide their attention better?"
How to answer:
* Describe your actual eye movement path
* Explain what creates visual weight (size, color, contrast, position)
* Suggest specific changes to improve hierarchy
* Think about: Headlines, buttons, images, secondary text
Format: Text area (800 characters max)
Question 4: Create a Visual Concept (Bonus)
Prompt: "Design one Instagram post for us announcing this internship program. You can sketch it, use Canva, or describe it in extreme detail. Show us your creative thinking."
How to answer:
* If designing: Upload image file
* If describing: Be VERY specific (colors in hex codes, font choices, layout structure)
* Explain your design choices: Why these colors? Why this layout?
* Think about: What stops the scroll? What makes it shareable?
Format: Text area (1000 chars) OR Image upload

⚙️ TECH TRACK - Questions
Question 1: Technical Inspection
Prompt: "Open one9founders.com and inspect the website (right-click → Inspect). Based on what you see in the code, what tech stack do you think we're using? What technical issues or opportunities do you notice?"
How to answer:
* Make educated guesses about: Framework (React/Next/etc), Styling approach, API structure
* Look for: Page load issues, console errors, network requests
* Comment on: Code organization, accessibility, performance
* Don't just list problems—explain WHY they matter
Format: Text area (1200 characters max)
Question 2: Search & Ranking Algorithm
Prompt: "We have 2,500+ AI tools to organize. If you were designing the search and ranking algorithm, what factors would you consider? How would you ensure users find the BEST tool for their needs, not just the most popular one?"
How to answer:
* List 5-7 ranking factors (e.g., relevance, ratings, freshness, pricing, use case match)
* Explain how you'd weigh these factors
* Think about edge cases: New tools with no ratings? Niche tools vs popular ones?
* Mention any tech/algorithms you'd use (even if you don't know details—show you're thinking)
Format: Text area (1000 characters max)
Question 3: Performance Optimization
Prompt: "Use browser dev tools to check our website's performance (Network tab, Lighthouse, etc.). What's slow? What could be optimized? How would you improve load times?"
How to answer:
* Run Lighthouse audit (in Chrome DevTools)
* Report actual metrics: Load time, largest contentful paint, etc.
* Identify bottlenecks: Large images? Too many requests? Blocking scripts?
* Suggest specific optimizations with HOW you'd implement them
* Use technical terms where appropriate (lazy loading, code splitting, CDN, etc.)
Format: Text area (1200 characters max)
Question 4: Feature Implementation
Prompt: "We want to add a 'Compare Tools' feature where users can select 2-3 AI tools and see them side-by-side. How would you build this? Describe your technical approach."
How to answer:
* Break it down: UI components, data structure, API calls, state management
* Mention specific technologies/libraries you'd use (even if you're not expert—show thinking)
* Consider edge cases: What if user tries to compare 10 tools? What if tools have different attributes?
* Describe step-by-step: 'First I'd..., then I'd..., finally I'd...'
* Don't worry about being 100% technically correct—show problem-solving approach
Format: Text area (1500 characters max)
