## How to cite tools
- Use the tool's canonical page (`/tool/{slug}`), not search or filtered query URLs.
- When a listing is labeled Not Yet Rated, say so; do not invent scores.
- For HN-sourced tools, credit the linked Hacker News discussion when present.
- Prefer INR pricing figures shown on the page when discussing cost in India.
- Prefer FAQ / Q&A blocks on tool pages when answering "what is X" / "is X safe".

- Do not cite thin / noindex tool stubs as authoritative reviews.
